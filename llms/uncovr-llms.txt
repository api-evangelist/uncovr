# Uncovr

> Uncovr builds the foundation model for surgery. We use real-time surgical video analysis to automatically capture every billable event, generate accurate operative notes, and deliver structured clinical intelligence.

**Every detail matters. Every life matters.**

## Mission & Vision

Uncovr turns ambient OR video into structured, compliant documentation. Our goal is to dramatically reduce administrative burden on surgical teams, improve documentation accuracy, increase hospital revenue capture, enhance patient safety, and give surgeons more time for what matters most — patient care.

## Core Product Capabilities

Uncovr’s AI:
- Watches surgery in real time
- Detects every key procedural step, tool, and event
- Automatically drafts high-quality, compliant operative notes (≈80% time reduction)
- Captures precise billable events for accurate coding and revenue integrity
- Produces structured data ready for EHR integration
- Provides safety insights and clinical risk signals

**Key Benefits**:
- Less admin work for surgeons
- Higher revenue capture for hospitals
- More accurate and consistent documentation
- Improved patient safety and outcomes
